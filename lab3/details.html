My Yoga Class

Going to yoga class is one of my favorite ways to exercise and relax. I enjoy taking time to focus on stretching, movement, and breathing.

My favorite part of yoga class is feeling relaxed and refreshed when the class is finished.

Yoga Poses I Enjoy

  • Child's Pose
  • Tree Pose
  • Mountain Pose
  • Downward-Facing Dog

I like practicing these poses because they help me stretch, relax, and focus.

My Class Routine

  1. Get ready and wear comfortable clothes.
  2. Bring my yoga mat and water.
  3. Follow the instructor during class.
  4. Take time to relax at the end of class.
