Why I Enjoy Yoga

Yoga is one of my favorite hobbies. Going to yoga helps me relax, stay active, and take a break from my daily routine. I enjoy going to class because it gives me time for myself and helps me feel comfortable.

I especially enjoy how calm and refreshed I feel after a yoga class.

Why Yoga Is Special to Me

  • It helps me relax.
  • It helps me stretch my body.
  • It can improve flexibility.
  • I enjoy learning new yoga poses.

How I Prepare for Yoga Class

  1. Wear comfortable clothes.
  2. Bring my yoga mat.
  3. Bring water.
  4. Get ready to relax and exercise.
